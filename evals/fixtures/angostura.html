THE RUM MAKING PROCESS

CREATING THE FAMOUS ANGOSTURA RUMS

Angostura® has been producing rum for more than 75 years and is now one of the most respected rum producers in the world. Our rums have gained the accolade of having the world’s most awarded rum range. Learn more about how our processes are fine tuned to make ‘liquid gold’, from fermentation, distillation, aging and blending, right up to bottling.

THE ART IN COOPERING

THE ART IN COOPERING

Coopering is a centuries’ old tradition in which wooden barrels, preferably oak, are prepared by trained craftsmen to be filled with alcohol and other liquids and allowed to age for a period of time. Learn more about the art of coopering here.
