Cellmaflex – Smarter Healthcare at Your Fingertips

Cellmaflex is an integrated healthcare platform in Trinidad and Tobago and expanding across Caribbean. It is designed to bring patients and providers together in one connected system. Purpose-built for effortless booking, engagement, and clinical efficiency, Cellmaflex goes beyond appointments to create a smooth and reliable healthcare journey. From consultation management to connected services, Cellmaflex ensures care is always accessible, organised, and digital-first.

What makes Cellmaflex unique is its adaptability for everyone in the healthcare ecosystem. Patients can book doctor appointments online with ease, while doctors and clinics can manage consultations and records seamlessly. Labs and imaging centres gain a direct channel to share results securely, and pharmacies can handle prescriptions in sync with doctors. By uniting doctors, patients, labs, imaging, and pharmacies under one platform, Cellmaflex creates a healthcare experience that works for everyone.  

The Complete Cellmaflex Ecosystem

Cellmaflex is more than just a way to book doctor appointments online – it is an integrated healthcare platform that connects every part of the care journey. Designed for different users but united through one connected system, Cellmaflex ensures that healthcare works seamlessly for everyone.
Cellmaflex for Patients

Patients can take charge of their health with easy access to care. From booking doctor appointments online to viewing prescriptions, lab results, and imaging reports, everything is available in one secure and convenient place.. 

Know More
Cellmaflex for Doctors & Clinics

Cellmaflex provides a complete clinic management software solution for healthcare providers across Trinidad and Tobago. Doctors and clinics can manage patient consultations, access medical records and keep appointment scheduling organised and hassle-free.

Know More
Cellmaflex Labs

Cellmaflex connects laboratories directly to doctors and to patients, creating an easier way to share and access test results safely. When manual processes and delays are removed, labs can provide quicker, more dependable diagnostics so that appropriate treatment decisions can be made.

Know More
Cellmaflex Pharmacy

Pharmacies benefit from direct digital access to doctors and to patients. Prescription management and fulfilment can be performed quickly, providing patients with timely access to pharmacies they trust.

Know More
Cellmaflex Imaging

Imaging centres can use Cellmaflex to give patients and doctors direct access to scans and reports. This integration increases accuracy, decreases delays, and guarantees that imaging results are available when needed for better coordinated care.

Know More

Why Choose Cellmaflex?

Regardless of where you fit in the healthcare continuum, Cellmaflex is designed with you in mind:

Patients

No more long lines! You can now easily book your appointment online, access your results and be in control of your health anytime, anywhere.

Doctors & Clinics

Keep your practice running smoothly with a clinic management software that makes patient care and scheduling effortless, while opening doors to higher practice efficiency and revenue growth.

Lab Technicians

Reduce paperwork by delivering results analysis faster and more accurately, all while liaising with doctors and patients and benefiting from smoother workflows that can bring in more service opportunities.

Imaging Technicians

Instantly share scans to minimise wait time and give patients and healthcare providers direct clarity with no delays while maximising utilisation of imaging services to boost profitability.

Pharmacists

Get digital orders, feel confident in preparation and get medication to patients more safely and quickly, with the added benefit of gaining more consistent prescription volumes that contribute to higher revenue.
