Payroll by hand

Five things that go quietly wrong.

None of them set off an alarm. You find out when NIB does, and by then it has been happening for months.

The wrong class

16 bands, and the gap between two of them can be a few dollars of weekly wage. $8,276 a month is Class X. $8,277 is Class XI.

Four weeks in a five week month

Contributions are weekly amounts. Some months hold five contribution weeks. Charge four and you are short.

Whoever started mid month

One day worked in a week is a full contribution week. Someone who joined on the 10th is not half a month.

Surcharge on the wrong people

Staff under 16 and staff 60 and over are exempt from health surcharge. Deduct it anyway and you are taking money you shouldn't.

Last year's rates

The rate rose to 16.2% in January 2026 and rises to 19.2% in January 2027. Every band moves. A spreadsheet doesn't know that.

Things employers say

“I gave him a raise and his NIS didn’t even move.”

NIS isn’t a percentage.

Each wage falls into one of 16 fixed weekly bands. Everyone inside the same band pays exactly the same. It only moves when a raise crosses into the next one.

Six of the 16 classes shown, weekly totals from the official NIBTT table effective 5 January 2026. Contributions stop rising once earnings pass $13,600 a month.

What else it handles

The awkward cases, already built in.

Part period staff

Enter the dates worked and their contribution weeks are counted from the calendar.

Four or five week months

Counted by the Mondays in the period, so the figures are right for that month.

Class Z

65 and over, under 16, or receiving retirement pension: employer only, no employee contribution.

Health surcharge exemptions

Under 16 and 60 and over are exempt. Applied automatically from the date of birth on file.

Overtime and bonuses

Add them and everything adjusts, including which class the person lands in that period.

Questions

The things people ask.

Does it replace my accountant?
No. Your accountant does your books and advises you. This does the repetitive calculation and paperwork, so nobody is paid to type numbers into forms every month.
Can my staff clock in or enter their own hours?
No. You enter the hours. We don’t collect hours from staff and there is no clocking in and out.
Do I need internet for it to work?
Only the first time you open it. After that it runs on your computer, and your staff details and wages never leave it.
What happens if my computer dies?
There is a backup button that saves everything to a single file. Keep it in Google Drive or on a memory stick. On a new computer you press restore and everything comes back.
Why is there a yearly fee?
Because the rates change. They rose in January 2026 and rise again in January 2027, and every earnings class moves with them. The licence keeps your system correct through those changes and covers support. Stop renewing and the software keeps working, it just stops getting rate updates.
I only have two or three staff. Is this worth it?
Possibly not yet. If you mostly want proper payslips, the payslip generator at $1,500 may suit you better. Tell us your situation and we will tell you honestly which one fits.
How long does setup take?
Usually a few days from when you send your business details and staff list. That includes loading everyone in and a training session, so you run your first payroll with us on the call.
